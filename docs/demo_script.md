# Demo video script (3 minutes)

Record the live web app at 1280x800, browser zoom 110%. Before recording, run Colab step 7b once so every scenario
opens instantly, and open the page a few minutes early so the readers are awake for a live run. Speak slowly; let the cards do the work.

## 0:00–0:15 Hook
On screen: the "Check a message" tab.
Say: "'We can probably hit the 15th if nothing else slips.' You mean: the deadline is at risk and I need a decision.
Most readers hear: relax, we're on track. Same words, a different action. Meaning Check shows you who will misread a
message before you send it."

## 0:15–0:50 Beat 1: who misreads, and how
Click the scenario **A deadline at risk, to a manager**. The stages light up and the cards fill in, then turn red or
green.
Say: "Sixteen readers on Meta's open-weight Muse Glimmer, in four countries, with two jobs and quick or careful
reasoning. Eight more on Muse Spark, through the Meta API, give a second opinion."

## 0:50–1:20 Beat 2: click a reader (the key moment)
Click a red **Ghana** card (any red card works; check in a rehearsal which ones are red live). The reader card opens.
Say: "This is exactly what happened. Here is the reader, in Ghana, and the exact prompt it was given. Here is what it
read. And here is how likely it found every reading: the red bar is what it would act on, the green one is what you
meant." Read the two percentages aloud. Press the right arrow to step through two more readers, then Esc.

## 1:20–1:55 Beat 3: the fix
Click **Fix it**.
Say: "An exact solver finds the fewest facts that rule out every wrong reading, and proves nothing shorter works. The
writer drafts four wordings; both models re-read every one; the shortest one they all understand is kept." Point at
the word diff, the "Wordings tried" table, and the tile for readers never used to build it. Open the Ghana card again:
the before and after bars sit side by side.

## 1:55–2:20 Beat 4: two Meta models disagree
Click **"Biweekly": two models disagree**. The Muse Glimmer row is green; the Muse Spark row turns red: "twice a week".
Say: "One model says this is fine. The other reads it the way many people do. Our pre-registered test found exactly
this, so every check now uses both." Click **Fix it**: both rows turn green.

## 2:20–2:45 Beat 5: evidence
Open the **Evidence** tab.
Say: "Pre-registered: on readers never used to build the fixes, understanding rose from 53 to 100 percent across 14
messages, and no message got worse. Muse Spark, which built none of those fixes, understood them 54 points more often
than the originals. And what did not work is listed too: a plain careful rewrite did as well on these tests, so the
value is the check itself." Click one row to show the wordings tried.

## 2:45–3:00 Close
Open **How it works** for two seconds (diagram and the audience theorem).
Say: "Built this week on Muse Glimmer and Muse Spark, on a mathematical theory of being understood. Every number
reproduces with one command. Meaning Check: say it once, and be understood."

## Notes
- AI agent prompts: if time allows, swap Beat 4 for **"Close it if it is resolved"** in the AI-agent group.
- Photo and Speak buttons (Muse Spark vision, Muse Voice Transcribe) can be shown in a second clip; do not rely on them
  live.
- Do not claim the readers are people: they are AI readers given backgrounds.
